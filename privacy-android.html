Privacy policy: Truelabel for Android

App: Truelabel (package com.saimshafi.truelabel) · Developer: Saim Shafi, Karachi, Pakistan · Last updated: 30 September 2026

Truelabel is a label-printing app. You type or import your products, and the app creates PDF labels on your phone. This policy explains what happens to data when you use the Android app. For the Shopify, Wix and WooCommerce versions, see the main privacy policy.

1. Your products and projects stay on your phone

Products, projects, label templates, settings and your monthly label count are saved only in the app's storage on your device. There is no Truelabel account and no Truelabel server. We never receive your product lists, prices, barcodes or files.

  • Files you import (CSV or Excel) are read on your phone and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • PDFs are created on your phone. They leave the phone only when you choose Share, Save or Print, and then go to the app, folder or printer you pick.
  • Backup files are created only when you tap "Back up projects" and are saved where you choose.
  • Android's own device backup may include the app's data if you have it turned on in your phone settings; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account.

2. Ads (Free plan only)

On the Free plan, the app shows small banner ads from Google AdMob. Paid plans have no ads, and ads are never shown on the PDF/print screen. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares, with Google, data used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

DataWhy
Device identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID)Serving and measuring ads, fraud prevention
IP address (can indicate approximate location)Serving ads, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads (views, taps)Ad measurement, analytics
Diagnostic information (app launch time, performance)Analytics, SDK performance

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Paid plans are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. To check which plan you have, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created by the app, your purchase records from Google Play (product, dates, status) and basic device information such as the app version and country. This is used only to unlock your plan and restore purchases. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

4. What we do not do

  • No sign-in, no contacts, no camera, no location permission, no photo access.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs other than the ad SDK described above.
  • We do not sell your data.

5. Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google Play and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Data inside the app is protected by Android's app sandbox.

6. Children

Truelabel is a business tool for shop owners and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete any project inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or using Android Settings → Apps → Truelabel → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Subscriptions are cancelled in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Google keeps its own purchase records under its policies.
  • To ask for the deletion of the anonymous purchase record kept by RevenueCat, email us from the app's Help page with your Google Play order number (GPA.xxxx). We will act within 30 days.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
