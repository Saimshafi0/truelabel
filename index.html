What it does

True-to-size printing

Every label is laid out in millimetres and exported as a PDF at the exact label size. A test page with a 100 mm ruler checks your printer scale.

Sheets and rolls

Presets for Avery sheets and DYMO, Zebra and Brother rolls, or enter any size. Start at any position to use half-used sheets.

Barcodes, QR codes, or both

EAN-13, UPC-A and Code 128, plus QR codes that open the product page. Thermal mode lines bars up with the print head.

Duplicate and missing barcodes

Finds products that share a barcode or have none, and creates unique codes saved straight to Shopify.

SKU generator

Build SKUs from a pattern like product, variant and number. Every SKU is checked so none repeat.

Locked templates

Save label layouts and lock them so a setting never changes by accident.

Print from your Products list

Select products in Shopify, choose Print barcode labels, and they open ready to print.

Sale price labels

Shows the compare-at price crossed out next to the sale price.

7 languages

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch, with your store currency.
