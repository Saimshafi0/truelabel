Privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the Truelabel Shopify app (“Truelabel”, “we”) accesses and stores when a merchant installs it, and how we use it. Contact: m.saimshafii@gmail.com.

Information we access

When you install Truelabel, Shopify lets the app read and update the data it needs to make labels:

  • Products and variants: titles, variant names, prices, compare-at prices, SKUs, barcodes, vendors, product types and handles.
  • Inventory quantities, to offer “match stock” label counts.
  • Basic store details: store address, currency and primary domain.

Truelabel updates only barcodes and SKUs, and only when you press a button that says it will save them to your products.

Truelabel does not access or store customer information such as names, emails, addresses or orders.

Information we store

WhatWhy
Store address and an access session issued by ShopifyTo keep the app signed in to your store
Label templates you save (names and layout settings)So you can reuse them
Number of labels printed each monthTo apply your plan's monthly limit

Product data is read from Shopify when you open the app and is not kept in our database. Label PDFs are created in your browser and are not uploaded to us.

How we use information

Only to provide the app's features, apply your plan and answer support requests. We do not sell, rent or share merchant data for advertising.

Service providers

Truelabel runs on a hosting provider that stores the data listed above on our behalf. Billing is handled by Shopify; we do not receive payment card details.

Retention and deletion

When you uninstall Truelabel, your access session is removed. Shopify sends a deletion request 48 hours later, and we then delete everything stored for your store, including templates and label counts. You can also ask us to delete your data at any time by email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information and to object to its processing. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. Requests Shopify forwards on behalf of your customers are answered automatically, since Truelabel stores no customer data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to the database is limited to what is needed to run the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced inside the app.
